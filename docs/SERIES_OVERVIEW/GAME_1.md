# GAME I

## The Boy Who Collected Silence

Shipped.

### Theme

Solitude

Listening

### Story Genre

Modern Philosophical Fairy Tale

### Game Genre

Interactive Fiction

### Reading Level

Read + Attentive

The first rung. The player is asked only to keep reading — and then quietly rewarded for reading closely.

Doomscrolling trains the mind to consume fast and discard faster.

This game trains the opposite: stay, notice, return.

The moon meter is the reading muscle. The hidden paragraphs are the first proof it works.

### Shape

20 chapters.

One path.

A bonus chapter for readers who found every hidden sentence.

### Gameplay

- Linear story
- Reflective choices
- Micro branching that always returns to the same road
- Hidden paragraphs, unlocked by attentive reading
- 5 hidden-sentence puzzles, optional, never blocking
- 8 keepsakes, collected by reading rather than searching
- A post-story keepsake puzzle, as a quiet reward
- One ending
- No inventory
- No combat
- No fail state

### What Choices Change

- reactions
- dialogue
- emotional framing
- which memories return later
- how warm a relationship becomes
- hidden lore

Never the main plot.

The destination stays.

The journey changes.

### Attentiveness

The story keeps a quiet count of how the player reads.

Rushing costs it.

Sitting with a scene restores it.

It is never shown as a number.

Only as a moon, changing phase.

Readers who stay receive sentences other readers never see.

### Product Goal

Teach players to enjoy reading again.

Then teach them to read closely.

Doomscrolling does not make people hate reading.

It makes them forget what it feels like to stay.

This game is the first reminder.

### Narrative Goal

Teach players to listen.

Not hearing.

Listening.

The noise in Bellwood is not metaphor.

It is a portrait of the world the player already lives in:

chatterboxes, bells, the inability to tolerate the space between words.

Embracing solitude is the first act of reclamation.

Learn that silence was never something to keep.

Only something to share.

### Position in the Arc

Game I is the entry point.

Before a person can reclaim focus, presence, or wisdom,

they first have to be able to sit with something long enough to feel it.

This game restores that capacity.

It is the quietest game in the series.

Also the most necessary.
