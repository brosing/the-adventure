# Studio Philosophy

We don’t make games about reading.

We make games that make people love reading.

We don’t teach people how to live.

We quietly invite them to rediscover it.

The world has a doomscrolling problem.

Not because people are broken.

Because everything around them competes for the part of attention that stories used to hold.

This series is a quiet attempt to hand that part back.

Game by game.

Philosophy by philosophy.

Without announcing itself.

Readers should never finish a chapter feeling smarter.

They should finish feeling quieter.
