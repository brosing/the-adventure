# Story Timeline

The games are released in order.

The world timeline does not have to be.

Example

```
Past
    ↓
The Keeper of One Flame
— "Before the bells came. Before anyone thought tending was beneath them."

    ↓

Present
The Boy Who Collected Silence
— "When the last silence wells were nearly dry."

    ↓

Near Future
The Girl Who Carried Time
— "After the boy's story spread, and before anyone had decided what it meant."

    ↓

Many Years Later
The Second Quiet
— "When the listening houses still had doors, but most of them were shut."
```

History should feel like legends.

Avoid explicit years.

Instead use expressions like:

> Before the Noise came.

> During the Long Silence.

> When the last lighthouse still shined.

> After the boy's jars were emptied.

> In the years when tending was still called work.
